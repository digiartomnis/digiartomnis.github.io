AGI for Game Experience: Turning Human Intent into Inhabitable Worlds

At DigiArtOmnis, we believe a game is not merely a collection of code, images, and rules, but an executable world capable of carrying human intention, emotion, and imagination. Future games and content will no longer be works produced in advance and delivered in fixed form; they will be worlds co-created by humans and intelligent agents, continually evolving through interaction.

We explore AGI for Game Experience: moving AI from generating content toward understanding experience. We translate the tacit, below-the-waterline expertise of game designers and artists—their judgments about gameplay, narrative, aesthetics, space, rhythm, and emotion—into capabilities that agents can learn, invoke, verify, and continuously evolve. Our goal is for AI not only to know how to make games, but to understand what makes play compelling, why it works, and why an experience truly moves people.

As AI continues to lower the cost of implementation, the truly scarce abilities will no longer be content-production skills, but the ability to define experience, express intention, and give a world meaning. AI can expand the power of human expression, but people will continue to determine a world's meaning and soul.

The Artistic Accessibility we pursue is not about enabling everyone to mass-produce more content. It is about giving every person the power to transform inner intention, emotion, and imagination into a world that others can enter, feel, and experience together.

We also support action-driven young researchers and creators through research mentorship, model and computing resources, and hands-on product development. So far, we have helped dozens of young talents conduct frontier AI research, build working prototypes, and publish at leading venues such as SIGGRAPH. We welcome collaborators working across computer graphics, multimodal agents, AI-native games, narrative and level design, digital art, and spatial design to explore the future of human expression and interactive worlds with us.
